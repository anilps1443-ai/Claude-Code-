# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audiences, confirmed by the owner: **lenders / financiers**, **fleet operators**, and **OEMs**. Each arrives at the site to judge whether TapFin's EV data intelligence is credible enough to book a demo.

The hero copy also names the "sustainability ecosystem" as a recipient of the output, but it was not chosen as a primary audience. Treat it as secondary until the owner says otherwise.

## Product Purpose

TapFin is an AI-powered EV data intelligence platform. Raw EV data (vehicle, battery, location and telemetry) goes in; intelligence for Lenders, OEMs, Operators and sustainability stakeholders comes out. The site exists to get qualified visitors to book a demo.

## Positioning

The differentiator, confirmed by the owner, is **AI risk and value scoring**: models that turn raw EV data into risk and value intelligence. Specific outputs (for example battery health or residual value) are illustrative and not confirmed.

## Operating Context

- The conversion event is **Book a Demo**. There is no self-serve sign-up or pricing in the codebase.
- The only surface today is one static hero in `index.html`. The preview toolbar (device switcher, replay, static state) is internal review tooling, not part of the visitor-facing product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing implementation: plain static HTML, CSS and JS in a single `index.html`; no framework or build step.
- Navigation names two products or sections, **FleetForce** and **Xchange**, plus About and News. What FleetForce and Xchange are has **not been confirmed**; do not define or describe them until the owner does.
- The "Book a Demo" buttons link to `#demo`, which does not exist yet. The demo-request destination is undecided.
- Terminology in use: Lenders, OEMs, Operators, Sustainability.
- Open: whether the site is only this hero or a fuller marketing site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **TapFin**.
- The colors in the CSS are marked in the code as placeholders, and the hexagon logo mark has not been confirmed as final. The owner says the final logo and brand colors exist but have not been supplied. Do not treat the current values as binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- **In the repository: none.** No customer names or logos, case studies, testimonials, metrics, data volumes or press.
- The owner says real customer logos and case studies, real metrics and data volumes, and the final logo and brand colors exist but have not been supplied yet.
- Until they are supplied, **do not fabricate** customers, statistics, testimonials, benchmarks or pricing.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the mechanism.** The claim is that raw EV data becomes risk and value intelligence; make that input-to-intelligence step understandable before asking for trust.
2. **One conversion.** Everything leads to booking a demo for lenders, fleet operators and OEMs.
3. **Proof is real or absent.** Use customer proof and metrics only once the owner supplies them; leave a gap rather than inventing.
4. **Say only what is confirmed.** Keep FleetForce and Xchange as names until they are defined, and keep outputs to what has been stated.
