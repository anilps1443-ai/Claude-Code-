02About Us

Who we are

TapFin is building what we believe the sustainability ecosystem has been missing - a unified layer that sits at the intersection of financing, asset intelligence and resale across the entire lifecycle.

Built by people who understand assets from the ground up, we combine real-world operations, data and AI to turn asset data into informed decisions.

03Why now?

EVs are not just vehicles. They are connected, data-rich assets.

Yet decisions across the asset lifecycle are still often made in silos:

Financing

Intelligence

Resale
